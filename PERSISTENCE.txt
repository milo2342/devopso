WCRP Development Utilities persistence

Recommended Railway volume mount: /data
The bot automatically uses /data/bot-data.json when /data exists.

Safety in v2.11.0:
- Startup backup
- Automatic backup every 6 hours
- Latest 10 automatic backups retained in /data/backups
- /data backup exports a sanitized user-downloadable JSON
- /data restore requires owner access + RESTORE confirmation and creates a pre-restore backup first

The packaged bot-data.json is only a sanitized restore seed. Existing /data/bot-data.json always takes priority.
