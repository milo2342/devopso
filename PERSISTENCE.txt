WCRP DATA PERSISTENCE

The bot now preserves and migrates existing bot-data.json records and writes a .bak backup before each save.

IMPORTANT FOR RAILWAY:
1. Add a persistent Railway Volume mounted at /data.
2. Set BOT_DATA_PATH=/data/bot-data.json
3. Redeploy once.

Without a persistent Volume, Railway may replace the service filesystem during deploys. No application code can preserve files that the hosting platform deletes.

Once /data is persistent, future bot deployments can update index.js/package.json without resetting authorizations, verification records, guild lists, staff records, tickets, or other bot data.
